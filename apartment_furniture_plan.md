## ROOM-BY-ROOM FURNITURE PLAN

### BEDROOM (11'0" x 11'5" = approx. 126 sq ft)

**Recommended Furniture**:

1. **Bed** (PRIORITY #1)
   - Size recommendation: Queen bed (60" x 80")
   - King would work but leaves less room for other furniture
   - Full/Double (54" x 75") if you want more floor space
   - Placement: Against the wall opposite the walk-in closet entrance
   - Frame with storage drawers underneath = bonus storage!

2. **Nightstands** (2)
   - Dimensions: 20"-24" wide, 24"-28" tall
   - Place one on each side of bed
   - Budget option: IKEA Hemnes ($79 each)
   - Mid-range: Target Threshold collection ($100-150 each)

3. **Dresser**
   - Dimensions: 48"-60" wide, 30"-36" tall
   - Placement: Along the wall with the window or near closet
   - Could skip if walk-in closet has enough built-in storage
   - Alternative: 2 smaller dressers (one for each of you)

4. **Lighting**
   - Table lamps for nightstands (2)
   - Floor lamp in corner if needed for reading
   - Consider smart bulbs for mood lighting

5. **Window Treatment**
   - Floor plan shows vertical blinds already installed
   - Optional: Add blackout curtains for better sleep (72" x 84" panels)
   - Curtain rod: 66"-120" adjustable

6. **Optional Items**:
   - Bench at foot of bed (storage bench ideal)
   - Full-length mirror (can mount on wall or door)
   - Small chair/reading nook in corner
   - Wall-mounted TV (24"-32") if you want one in bedroom

**Layout Tips**:
- Keep at least 24" walking space on each side of bed
- Leave 36" clearance in front of dresser for opening drawers
- Position bed to face door but not directly in line with it
- Use vertical space - wall shelves, hooks

**Estimated Budget**:
- Bed frame: $300-800
- Mattress: $500-1,500 (Queen)
- Nightstands: $150-300 (pair)
- Dresser: $200-500
- Bedding: $150-300
- Lamps: $60-150
- **Total: $1,360 - $3,550**

### LIVING/DINING AREA (12'3" x 17'4" = approx. 213 sq ft)

This is your largest space - open concept means smart furniture placement is crucial!

**LIVING ROOM SECTION**

1. **Sofa** (PRIORITY #2)
   - Size: 72"-84" (6-7 feet) - anything larger overwhelms the space
   - Placement: Facing the TV/entertainment area, back to kitchen
   - Consider apartment-size or loveseat + chair combo
   - L-shaped sectional could work but measure carefully!
   
2. **Coffee Table**
   - Dimensions: 36"-48" long, 18"-24" wide
   - Leave 14"-18" between sofa and coffee table
   - Recommendation: Rectangular or oval (better flow than square)
   - Storage underneath is a huge plus in smaller spaces

3. **TV Stand / Entertainment Center**
   - Dimensions: 48"-60" wide, 20"-24" tall
   - Placement: Against wall opposite sofa
   - TV size: 43"-55" max (don't go bigger - too close)
   - Look for storage cabinets/shelves for media, games, etc.

4. **Side Table / End Table**
   - Dimensions: 18"-22" wide
   - Place next to sofa (opposite side from dining)
   - Great for lamps, drinks, books

5. **Area Rug**
   - Size: 5'x7' or 6'x9' to define living space
   - Should fit under front legs of sofa and coffee table
   - Helps separate living from dining visually

6. **Floor Lamp or Table Lamp**
   - Essential for ambient lighting beyond overhead
   - Place in corner or next to sofa

**DINING AREA SECTION**

1. **Dining Table** (PRIORITY #3)
   - Size: 36" x 48" (seats 4) or round 42"-48" diameter
   - Placement: Near kitchen, leaving clear path to balcony
   - Extendable table = great for hosting guests!
   - Round tables work better in tight spaces

2. **Dining Chairs** (4)
   - Standard dining height: 18" seat height
   - Can mix 2 chairs + bench on one side to save space
   - Stackable or folding chairs can be stored when not needed

3. **Buffet/Console Table** (Optional)
   - If space allows: 40"-50" long, 16" deep
   - Great for serving food, extra storage
   - Alternative: Bookshelf or storage unit

**Layout Strategy for Open Concept**:

**Option A: Traditional Layout**
```
[BALCONY DOORS]
    |
[DINING TABLE]
    |
[AREA RUG]
[SOFA] → facing → [TV STAND]
    |
[KITCHEN]
```

**Option B: Floating Sofa Layout**
```
[BALCONY DOORS]
    |
[DINING TABLE]
    |
[SOFA] (floating in middle, back to dining)
    ↓ facing
[TV STAND on wall]
    |
[KITCHEN]
```

**Measurements to Consider**:
- Allow 30"-36" walkway between dining table and walls/furniture
- Keep 3 feet clear path from kitchen to balcony door
- Sofa should be 12"-18" from wall (if floating) or against wall
- TV viewing distance: 6-8 feet for 50" TV

**Estimated Budget**:
- Sofa: $500-1,500
- Coffee table: $100-300
- TV stand: $150-400
- TV: $300-600 (50" smart TV)
- Side table: $50-150
- Dining table: $200-500
- Dining chairs (4): $200-600
- Area rug: $100-300
- Lamps: $80-200
- **Total: $1,680 - $4,550**

### HOME OFFICE AREA

**WHERE TO PUT IT**:

**Option 1: Bedroom Corner**
- Pro: Separate from living space, quiet for calls
- Con: Takes up bedroom space, harder to work late

**Option 2: Dining Table Doubles as Desk**
- Pro: Saves money and space
- Con: Have to clear for meals, no permanent setup

**Option 3: Living Room Corner** (RECOMMENDED)
- Pro: Natural light, can watch TV on breaks
- Con: Less privacy for video calls (backdrop)

**Essential Office Furniture**:

1. **Desk**
   - Dimensions: 48"-60" wide, 24"-30" deep
   - Height: 29"-30" for standard desk
   - Recommendation: Simple modern desk with cable management
   - Budget: IKEA Malm ($179), Linnmon/Adils combo ($85)
   - Mid-range: Target Room Essentials ($150-250)

2. **Office Chair** (PRIORITY - you'll sit here a LOT!)
   - Ergonomic with lumbar support
   - Adjustable height and armrests
   - Budget: $100-150
   - Recommended: $200-400 range for your back health
   - Examples: Secretlab, Herman Miller (if splurging), IKEA Markus

3. **Monitor Setup**
   - External monitor: 24"-27" (or dual monitors)
   - Monitor arm/stand to raise to eye level
   - Reduces neck strain significantly

4. **Storage**
   - Small filing cabinet or drawer unit
   - Desk organizer for supplies
   - Cable management (clips, sleeves, box)

5. **Lighting**
   - Desk lamp with adjustable arm
   - Warm white or daylight bulb (not harsh blue)
   - Ring light if doing video calls

6. **Sound/Privacy**
   - Headphones with mic for calls
   - Consider small room divider or bookshelf as backdrop for video

**Estimated Budget**:
- Desk: $150-400
- Chair: $200-400
- Monitor: $150-300
- Desk accessories: $100-200
- Lighting: $40-100
- **Total: $640 - $1,400**

## STYLE CONSIDERATIONS

**Questions to Discuss Together**:
1. What's your overall aesthetic? (Modern, traditional, eclectic, minimalist, boho?)
2. Color palette? (Neutral with pops of color, all white, dark/moody, colorful?)
3. Wood tones? (Light/natural, medium/walnut, dark/espresso, painted white?)
4. Metal finishes? (Gold/brass, silver/chrome, black, mixed metals?)
5. Patterns? (Solids only, subtle patterns, bold prints?)

**Creating Cohesion**:
- Pick 2-3 main colors and repeat throughout
- Stick to 1-2 wood tones
- Choose one metal finish as primary
- Mix textures (soft fabrics, hard wood, metal, plants)
- Let one room flow into the next with shared colors

## NOTES & TIPS

**General Tips**:
- **Vertical space** - Use wall shelves, tall bookcases to maximize storage
- **Multi-functional** - Ottoman with storage, murphy desk, extendable table
- **Light colors** - Make small spaces feel larger
- **Mirrors** - Reflect light, make rooms feel bigger
- **Under-furniture** - Bed risers create storage space underneath
- **Over-door** - Hooks and organizers for extra storage

**Common Mistakes to Avoid**:
- Buying furniture before measuring
- All furniture against walls (float some pieces!)
- Matching sets (mix styles for character)
- Ignoring lighting (overhead alone is harsh)
- Furniture too large for space
- Buying everything at once (live in space first!)
- Forgetting about doorway clearance

**Connecticut-Specific**:
- Winter heating - Don't block radiators or vents
- Snow storage - Boot tray, coat hooks near door
- Cold weather - Area rugs for warmth, blackout curtains for insulation
